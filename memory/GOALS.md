## Current Iteration Goals

**Current Milestone**: M5 — Rhythm Control & Couple Experience

**Objective**: Enforce a three-period rhythm with no more than two activities per period, then deepen the couple-oriented copy and experience.

### Tasks for This Iteration

1. Add backend rhythm validation for morning / afternoon / evening, with a maximum of two activities per period.
2. Add explicit downtime or breathing-room activities between dense segments.
3. Strengthen `add_couple_touch` so copy varies by activity type, destination, and period.
4. Build or refine the frontend `CoupleMessage` / quote presentation.

### Acceptance Criteria

- Every day is grouped into morning / afternoon / evening with at most two activities per period.
- Dense periods include explicit breathing room or a slow-travel note.
- Every activity has a warm, non-generic couple note matched to its type and destination.
- The UI presents couple notes with the established romantic visual language.
